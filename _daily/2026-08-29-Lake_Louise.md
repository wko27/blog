YESTERDAY:
* drove up to Banff!
  * about 1.5 hours away
  * picked up sandwiches at a cute cafe
* went to Bow Lake first
  * lovely lunch by the lake
  * got attacked by a bunch of flies
* drop by Lake Peyto next
  * apparently Peyto was an explorer
  * did a fun hike over a mountain to look back at Bow Lake
  * mistook the incline of 250m for 60m
* head back to Banff to check into hotel
  * they included a free local bus pass for 3 days
* found a restaurant serving a tomahawk bison steak!
  * got seated right away
  * sirloin steak was better than the bison
* wandered around downtown looking at gift shops
* bit of work
* got to sleep around 1 am

TODAY:
* woke up around 8 am
* head out to see a waterfall recommended by the waitress from dinner yesterday
  * Takakkaw Falls
  * beautiful waterfall and the sun came out briefly
* drove to Lake Louise
  * lunch at the resort cafeteria
  * BLT wasn't too bad
  * vegan brownie was way too sweet
* took a bus over the actual Lake Louise
  * cheapest bus tickets are released a few days in advance and sell out immediately
  * luckily, Alisa bought us a few tickets earlier
* did a short hike to look down on the lake from a scenic outlook
* took a connector bus over to Lake Moraine
  * sun came out just as we got to the top of a rock pile
  * beautiful views and vibrant colors
* head back to car parked at the resort
* dropped by Johnston Canyon on the way back
  * catwalks and a cave where you can see a waterfall up close!
* got back to Banff after sunset
* found an Indian restaurant for dinner
  * only had to wait 20 mins to get a table
  * staff was very patient with my a1 milk protein allergy
  * tried a new Indian dish called a moilee
* walked around picking up more souvenirs
* decided to walk back to the hotel

LEARNINGS:
* moilee is pretty tasty!
* melt from glaciers pours a 'glacial flour' into the lakes
  * this absorbs lots of wavelengths leaving a vibrant blue behind
